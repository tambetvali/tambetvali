### Fenja & Menja — Song of Endless Mill of Fate  
[![Fenja & Menja — Song of Endless Mill of Fate](https://img.youtube.com/vi/_3eaPANn6uo/0.jpg)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_3eaPANn6uo)

**Context & Meaning:**  
This song retells the myth of **Fenja and Menja**, giant maidens bound to the mill **Grotte**,  
whose grinding determines the **fate of kings**, **wars**, and **the sea itself**.  
It is one of the deepest mythic motifs: **fate as labor**, **cosmos as mill**,  
and **the world turning through suffering and prophecy**.

**Musical Lineage:**  
- Nordic epic folk  
- Slow‑drum Valhalla style  
- Fate‑chant tradition  
- Echoes of *Grottasöngr* (The Song of the Mill)

**Cultural Ties:**  
This belongs to the **Eddic poem family**, connecting directly to the **Poetic Edda**.
